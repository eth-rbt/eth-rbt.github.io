Stoove

Redesigning a portable stove. 2026.

As of 2023, 49 percent of the market for portable stove uses were domestic or urban, while the portable stove form factor has remained unchanged for the past 80 years - the coleman stove briefcase form factor. This project is a redesign of the coleman stove to make it suitable for uban use, like a picnic or backyard cooking.
Stoove is a stove stacking burner, storage, and fuel in a bento box form factor. It comes with a pivotable gas line that connects the burner to the fuel tank, and a storage middle layer that stores a grill, a pan, and a collaspable kettle. The design comes with a built in windguard for the burner, and a simple setup process.

Collaborator: Abdullatif Al Alsheikh, Cesar Meza
